It all started eons ago. The main cause of the Outerversal War, known to some entities as Flame Out, was Project Unity.

Project Unity had a faction called "???", or "End". Its leader, Termintryhaos, left his own universe to conquer others and established his base, Necrostasis. He built a super-weapon, "Endgame", based on the "Advance" design and capable of destroying the universe, and later made smaller "Endgame" MK-2 versions.

A coalition fought against the End. It made use of a "prison": Apathy, with Empathy sealed inside. Both were used as weapons, and Empathy would emerge whenever Apathy was threatened. Those who fought in that war gave it everything, and one by one they burned out until only ashes were left. Apathy was built to be those ashes: everything that remains after the fire, when there is nothing left to feel. Empathy was sealed inside it as the last ember.

The war ended abruptly with the death of Project Unity's emperor, killed by his own creation. Nothing faded and nothing was negotiated; the fire simply went out. That is why the survivors call it Flame Out. This fatal mistake took most of the End's opponents with it, while the End faction survived and lived on as an independent power. The fallen's bodies were annihilated, distorted or shifted. Their form isn't supported in this sector of reality, and only the Kami holds them together. The survivors either died from the forces of the End or were stranded in this reality.

But one survived, though distorted. He was burned out like the rest, with nothing left to fight for, and he had watched the Kami erase his kind. He alone managed to slip past her hold and found a world where he could hide. It was called "Terraria". He couldn't fit directly into it, so he created a layer between Terraria's borders and the endless universe. Soon he took the title of Watcher and a strange name: "HE".

HE remembered Apathy and tried to create the same, but failed. His version was imperfect, weak and dysfunctional compared to the original. Yet for the world of Terraria, even that was a complete apocalypse. It destroyed and burned so many that HE had to give it a purpose: to make it a true "prison", like the original. He created an entity called Empathy and trapped it inside. Its powers were destructive, yet the complete opposite of Apathy's, and because of this, Apathy could no longer destroy as it had.

After the war, the original Apathy wanders the universe aimlessly, waiting until it can release the force inside it. The original Empathy, sealed within, waits for its turn too.

The "Weakening": before it, the force inside Apathy might have been able to compete with the End. But the original Empathy did not want to be just a weapon, and it refused to burn out completely. So it weakened itself inside Apathy. Now it can only get out if Apathy is completely destroyed.

Despondency was a new project of the End faction, a younger version of Endgame. It used brute force rather than the "prison" concept, and was scattered across the cosmos to annihilate and demoralize those who oppose the End.

The Kami is an unidentified force that holds the affected together and is on their side. She is Project Unity's own creation, and her purpose was to save the distorted, even if, to her, saving meant releasing what could not be mended. She still exists because HE has not yet been captured.

The layer from which HE watches is called the "Void". No entity besides him can exist there, not even powers such as the Kami, or Termintryhaos himself.

The so-called "tapes" were nothing but a false story told by HE. His Empathy and Apathy are not vessels; they are a reminder of a terrible mistake. The "Apathetic" and "Empathetic" worlds were created by Apathy and Empathy themselves. HE made them by his own design, and they created their own worlds in order to exist, just as he built his layer in order to exist.

The Kami is still looking for HE, and she grows more furious. She knows HE still exists. She created a master machine for hunting and killing: Yggdrasil. In the end, Yggdrasil should have erased Terraria; however, you defeated HE first. You destroyed his creations and freed the world from destruction and complete extinction. The Kami called Yggdrasil back, and it became one of her servants. She is calm now, and what remains of the war is only its remnants: the originals, Apathy and Empathy, and the war's other participants, each still in place. Against them, too, you would stand no chance.

You freed your own world; there is no need to come outside. You wouldn't last a second against the End. Now your purpose ends here. From now on, this is your own world, with your own rules and control. Farewell.
